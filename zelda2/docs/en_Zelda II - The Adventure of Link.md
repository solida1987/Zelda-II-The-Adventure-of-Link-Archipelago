# Zelda II: The Adventure of Link

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the
options you need to configure and export a config file.

## What does randomisation do to this game?

Every fixed pickup in Hyrule is replaced by an item from the multiworld: the
eight key items, the Heart and Magic Containers, palace keys, experience bags,
jars, extra lives, and the three quest items townspeople trade for spells.
Where you find them no longer tells you what they are.

Enemy drops are left alone. Only pickups that sit in a fixed place, and can be
taken once, become locations.

## What is the goal?

The vanilla ending: return all six crystals, reach the Great Palace, and beat
Dark Link.

## What items can be in another player's world?

All of them. Key items, containers, keys, experience and filler can all be
found by other players, and their items can be found in yours.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

Items you find in your own game are already in your ROM when the seed is
patched, so they appear as normal.

Items sent to you from other worlds are not written into the running game yet,
so this world should be played single-player until that is fixed.

## What versions of the game are supported?

The NES release, USA. Every other release of Zelda II — the GameCube
Collector's Edition, the Game Boy Advance Classic NES Series, Virtual Console
and Nintendo Switch Online — is this same ROM running in an emulator.
